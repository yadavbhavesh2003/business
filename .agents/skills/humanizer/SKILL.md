# Humanizer — Authentic MSME Tone & Copy

## Rules
1. Write concise, actionable English and authentic Unicode Gujarati (ગુજરાતી).
2. Avoid corporate fluff, robotic cadence, or marketing buzzwords.
3. Error messages and system prompts should speak directly to counter cashiers, godown workers, and CAs.
