# Bharat Accounting & Gujarati i18n

## Rules
1. Format currency using Indian grouping: ₹ 12,34,567.00 (Lakhs & Crores).
2. Legal amounts in words: English ('Rupees ... Only') and Gujarati ('રૂપિયા ... ફક્ત').
3. Authentic Unicode Gujarati numerals and text without roman transliteration.
4. Indian Financial Year computation (April 1 to March 31).
